Animas: Ember

A turn-based creature-collecting RPG in the making: built with Godot 4, drawn in pixels, and open source. Explore the starter town right here in your browser.

▶ Play in your browserDownload

Pre-alpha: explore, meet all 151 animas, and get challenged by trainers. Battles are next.

Click the game, then use the arrow keys and Enter

Built like the classics, made from scratch

Familiar handheld-RPG feel, with every tile, sprite, and letter of text made for this game.

151 original animas

Twelve types and 71 evolution families, each with a hand-built sprite, stats, and a dex entry.

Trainers who spot you

Cross a trainer’s line of sight and they exclaim, walk up, and challenge you. Wild animas hide in tall grass.

Classic grid movement

Tap to turn, hold to walk, one tile at a time, in a pixel-perfect 240×160 view.

Original art and font

The tileset, 31 character looks, every anima, and the Animas Pixel typeface are made from scratch. No borrowed assets.

Plays everywhere

Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, and this browser, across x86, x64, arm32, and arm64 chips.

Touch ready

On phones and tablets an on-screen D-pad and action button appear automatically.

Open source

Built in the open by Pixel Pulse Games. Read the code, file an issue, or send a pull request.

Easy to extend

Maps from simple text layouts, NPC dialogue in the inspector, and automated tests for the core loop.

151 animas, every one with its own look

Twelve types, 71 evolution families, and a hand-built sprite for each. Filter by type and tap one to meet it.

Everyone has a face

The hero, the rival, every villager, and every trainer get their own hair, outfit, and accessories: 31 unique looks.
