# Animas: Ember v0.1.0: 151 animas and a world full of faces

Phase 2 is here. The world now has **151 original animas**, and every one has its own hand-built look. Every hero, villager, and trainer has a unique look too. Cross a trainer's line of sight and they will walk up and challenge you, and wild animas now hide in the tall grass.

## Highlights

- **151 animas** in 71 evolution families across 12 types, each with its own sprite, stats, evolution chain, and dex entry
- **The Animadex:** press Esc (or tap DEX on a touch screen) to browse all 151, with stats and info pages
- **Trainers with line of sight:** they spot you, exclaim, walk up, and challenge you. Beat them and they remember. (Real battles arrive in a later update, so challenges count as wins for now.)
- **Wild encounters** in tall grass, with per-map encounter tables and an encounter screen
- **31 unique character looks:** the hero, rival, villagers, and every trainer have their own hair, outfit, and accessories
- **Route 1:** a new map east of town with three trainers and wild grass
- **Portable by default:** new `-portable` archives and tiny `-setup` installers keep your saves in a `data` folder next to the game and write nothing to AppData. Copy the folder and your progress comes with you
- **A redesigned website** with an interactive gallery of all 151 animas, a character showcase, and a playable demo that now includes Route 1 and its trainers

## Try it

Play instantly in your browser at https://pixel-pulse-games.github.io/Animas-Ember/, or pick your platform from the downloads below. Builds are not yet code-signed, so expect a security prompt on first run (see the README for how to get past it).
