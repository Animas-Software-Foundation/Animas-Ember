# Animas: Ember v0.2.0: 151 animas, trainers, and saves that travel with you

This release brings the world to life and lets you keep your progress. There are now **151 original animas**, every character has a unique look, trainers challenge you on sight, and the game **saves and continues** automatically. It is also **portable**: your saves live in a folder next to the game, never in AppData.

## Highlights

- **Save and Continue:** the game saves automatically (changing maps, beating trainers, meeting new animas, closing the game) and picks up where you left off next time. A new **menu** (Esc, or the MENU button on touch screens) has Animadex, Save game, New game, and Resume
- **151 animas** in 71 evolution families across 12 types, each with its own sprite, stats, evolution chain, and dex entry, all browsable in the **Animadex**
- **Trainers with line of sight:** they spot you, exclaim, walk up, and challenge you. Beat them and they remember. (Real battles arrive in a later update, so challenges count as wins for now.)
- **Wild encounters** in tall grass, with per-map encounter tables and an encounter screen
- **31 unique character looks:** the hero, rival, villagers, and every trainer have their own hair, outfit, and accessories
- **Route 1:** a new map east of town with three trainers and wild grass
- **Portable by default:** new `-portable` archives and `-setup` installers (a graphical wizard on Windows) keep your saves in a `data` folder next to the game and write nothing to AppData. Copy the folder and your progress comes with you
- **A redesigned website** with an interactive gallery of all 151 animas, a character showcase, and a playable demo that includes Route 1 and its trainers

Play instantly in your browser at https://pixel-pulse-games.github.io/Animas-Ember/, or pick your platform from the downloads below. Builds are not yet code-signed, so expect a security prompt on first run (see the README for how to get past it).
