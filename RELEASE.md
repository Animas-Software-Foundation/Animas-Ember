# Animas: Ember v0.0.1: The overworld foundation

First pre-alpha release. Walk around a small town, step through a door into a house, talk to NPCs and signs, and wander into tall grass. Creatures and battles come next.

## Highlights

- Grid movement with tap-to-turn and hold-to-walk, plus tile collision
- Door warps with fade transitions
- Typewriter dialogue in the new hand-drawn *Animas Pixel* font
- Original 16x16 tileset with collision pre-configured
- On-screen touch controls on phones and tablets
- Builds for Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android, plus an iOS Xcode project when enabled

## Try it

Play instantly in your browser at https://pixel-pulse-games.github.io/Animas-Ember/, or pick your platform from the downloads below. Builds are not yet code-signed, so expect a security prompt on first run (see the README for how to get past it).

Full details in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/pixel-pulse-games/Animas-Ember/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
